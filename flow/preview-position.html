One Flow to a Funded Position

Traders pick an outcome and size a position. The flow handles the shortfall, the routing across chains and the buy itself, so the run ends at a position they own rather than a balance they still have to spend.

1 · Fund

The wallet is empty — $0.00 on Citrea. One tap on Deposit and Nexus has already picked the route: three assets across Ethereum, Optimism and MegaETH, auto-selected to cover the $20. Deposit Now, and the balance arrives.

2 · Predict

Nothing is chosen yet — both outcomes inert, the summary blank. A tap on No 83¢ resolves the card around that side, and MAX puts the whole balance behind it: 24.09 shares, 20.00 ctUSD, $23.26 to win.

3 · Trade

Cast bet. The button goes to work, and the position lands — 24.09 No shares at 83¢. The run started with an empty wallet and ends owning something.

Fund · Predict · Trade — one run, cut three ways, looping. 8.7s · 6.9s · 7.6s. Recorded from flow/player-prediction.html at 2× against the five numbered screens on the Paper page “Prediction Market”, on the same 821×537 panel as the trading-balance film.
  • Trade continues the exact card Predict ends on, so it is recorded without the opening rise and that join is invisible. Fund and Predict each open somewhere new, so they keep theirs — the rise now reads as a chapter starting rather than a stutter.
  • $20 is the only dollar figure in the film, and MAX does the sizing: after the deposit the balance is 20.00 ctUSD, so one tap on the chip as drawn puts the whole balance on the bet. No invented quick-amount.
  • Four numbers were changed from the artboards to survive a loop — see the notes handed back with this build.
